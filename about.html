About Toding's Empanada

Toding's Empanada is a food cart business that offers crispy, tasty, and freshly prepared empanadas at affordable prices.

We want to give our customers delicious snacks that they can enjoy with family and friends.

Our Mission

To serve delicious, affordable, and quality empanadas that bring happiness to every customer.

Our Vision

To become a trusted local empanada brand known for great taste, friendly service, and customer satisfaction.
